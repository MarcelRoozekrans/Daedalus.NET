The phase ran subagent-driven on `phase/3.2-reflection-out` from `main` `4091c648`, across three repositories: Thalos.NET,
AI.Sentinel and Daedalus. Each task had a fresh implementer and a fresh reviewer, except where a ruling below says the
controller reviewed a small diff itself. The plan was scanned against the spec before any task ran, and the twenty
findings of that scan, F1 to F20, were ruled on first. Every decision taken on the owner's behalf is listed below
with what it costs if it is wrong, so it can be checked and reversed.

## Owner decisions

- **AI.Sentinel ships the fix on its 2.x line as 2.4.0.** Reversed the same day, below.
